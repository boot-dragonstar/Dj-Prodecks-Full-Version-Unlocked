## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Professional DJ Mixer** — Utilize a crossfader to seamlessly blend tracks and create smooth transitions between songs.
- **Multi-Format Compatibility** — Supports multiple audio formats including MP3, OGG, WAV, and FLAC, ensuring versatile use across different music files.
- **3-Band Equalizer** — Adjust sound frequencies for each audio channel, giving you control over your mix's tonal quality.
- **AutoMix Function** — Effortlessly create perfect mixes by allowing the software to automatically transition between tracks.
- **CUE Points and Loops** — Save and store up to 3 CUE points or loops per song, enabling quick access to key moments in your tracks.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Integrated Effects** — Enhance your mixes with three audio effects: echo, flanger, and wah-wah, adding depth and creativity to your performances.
- **Scratching Feature** — Perform scratching using integrated jog-wheel controllers, mimicking the style of professional DJs.
- **Load Audio Samples** — Import and manage up to three audio samples for real-time mixing and creativity.
- **ASIO Driver Compatibility** — Ensure low-latency audio playback through compatibility with ASIO drivers, improving overall performance.
